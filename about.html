THE PEOPLE BEHIND ANAVA

Creative vision paired with structured production execution.

FOUNDER • CREATIVE DIRECTOR • PRODUCER • DIRECTOR

JACKSON KHATRI

Jackson’s journey began with a B.Sc. in Animation & Multimedia, developing an early understanding of visual storytelling, frame composition, and bringing ideas to life frame by frame.

Working across assistant direction, post-production, scripting, producing and directing, he formed one core belief:

"A great production starts much before the camera starts rolling."

That core belief became Anava Films — turning client briefs into visual stories worth making.

EXECUTIVE PRODUCER • PRODUCTION HEAD

ANJAN KHATRI

As Anava grew, Jackson’s brother Anjan Khatri joined the company as Executive Producer to strengthen the production muscle of the business.

Anjan turns ambitious creative decks into streamlined, high-efficiency shoots — managing budgets, logistics, and crew coordination like a well-oiled clock.

"Turning creative vision into seamless, stress-free production execution."

Together, Jackson and Anjan bring creative direction and production muscle under one roof.
