LET’S TALK
OUR END-TO-END MODEL

THINK · MAKE · FINISH

Instead of disjointed vendor handoffs, we bring the creative and executional sides of filmmaking under one roof. Here is what we do across each stage.

THINK

01 / CONCEPT
  • ✦
    Creative Direction
    Finding the distinct visual voice, tone, and storytelling hook that positions the brand above category noise.
  • ✦
    Ideation & Concept Development
    Turning raw thoughts, problems or products into sharp, memorable, and shootable concepts.
  • ✦
    Scripting
    Crafting dialog, voiceover, shot treatments, and director decks that give the idea its exact rhythm and emotion.
  • ✦
    Creative Consulting
    Brands can approach us even before they have a production brief. We help define what the idea could be before deciding how it should be made.

MAKE

02 / SHOOT
  • ✦
    Production
    End-to-end shoot management, scheduling, equipment, budgeting, crew assembly, and smooth on-set execution.
  • ✦
    Direction
    Translating the creative deck into striking camera movements, pacing, and authentic on-screen performances.
  • ✦
    Casting
    Our network has expanded beyond domestic talent, allowing us to work with international talent when the story, brand or campaign requires it.
  • ✦
    Art Direction
    We build the visual world around the idea — from production design and props to styling, colour, set details and the smallest elements within the frame.

FINISH

03 / POST
  • ✦
    Offline Editing
    Narrative rhythm, cut pacing, and structural storytelling that hooks attention and maintains momentum.
  • ✦
    Music & Sound
    Custom sonic identities, bespoke scores, foley, voiceover mastering, and cinematic sound design.
  • ✦
    Colour Grading & Online
    For high-end grading and online finishing, we collaborate with leading post-production partners including Prime Focus, Famous, Splice Studios, and Nube (Grading Studio). The post-production partner can vary depending on the project's creative and technical requirements.
  • ✦
    VFX & Mastering
    With a foundation in Animation & Multimedia, Anava has developed relationships with a strong network of VFX and animation artists across disciplines — Compositing, Cleanup, Animation, VFX, Motion work, Finishing. We then take the final film through mastering and deliver it in the formats required across platforms.
